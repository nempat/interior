A few helpful answers

Before we begin.

A few things to know when planning your home interiors.

How accurate is the calculator?

It provides an indicative planning range using simplified assumptions. Site measurements, materials and final scope may change the cost.

Can I design selected rooms only?

Yes. Use the room counters to include only the spaces you want to plan.

Does this include every possible cost?

No. Ask the actual company to confirm inclusions, exclusions, taxes, installation and other charges in a written quotation.

What happens when I request a quote?

This demo opens WhatsApp with your selections pre-filled. It does not automatically book an appointment or store your details.
